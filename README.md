# FleetPro Logistics

## Vehicle Maintenance Management System

**Project Type:** Business Analysis + Systems Analysis + Software Development  
**Project Status:** Business Analysis Complete — Systems Analysis In Progress

---

## 1. Project Overview

FleetPro Logistics is a portfolio case study focused on analysing and improving a vehicle maintenance-recording process for a logistics company operating a fleet of more than 300 trucks.

The project follows an end-to-end approach, beginning with the identification of a business problem and progressing through Business Analysis, Systems Analysis, Software Development, Testing and Deployment.

The project is designed to demonstrate how business needs can be translated into system requirements and ultimately into a working software solution.

## 2. Business Problem

FleetPro's vehicle maintenance information has historically been managed through a combination of physical vehicle files, Excel spreadsheets and communication channels such as WhatsApp and email.

As the fleet grew to more than 300 trucks, the existing maintenance-recording process became increasingly difficult to manage and track consistently.

This resulted in challenges including:

- Difficulty retrieving vehicle maintenance history
- Incomplete historical maintenance information
- Reduced visibility of vehicle maintenance status
- Difficulty verifying whether maintenance had already been performed
- Increased risk of repeated or unnecessary maintenance
- Increased difficulty managing maintenance information as the fleet grew

The investigation identified that the underlying problem was not simply the use of paper records or spreadsheets, but the lack of a formal and scalable maintenance-recording process.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

The project aims to design and develop a solution that can:

- Centralise vehicle maintenance information
- Maintain vehicle maintenance history
- Standardise the maintenance-recording process
- Record maintenance costs
- Improve maintenance visibility
- Improve retrieval of vehicle maintenance information
- Support fleet and information growth
- Support maintenance and financial reporting
